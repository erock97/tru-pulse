# Day 2: first-conversation onboarding

Eric's September 14 objective is confidence receiving a Zillow Preferred lead and
handling the first call from beginning to end. Start with introduction and agenda,
teach before practice, use ALMS, and keep material available for later reference.
Do not import the entire sales process. First-showing delivery, post-showing
discovery, lender training, and later transaction work belong in other courses.

The newer unanswered-lead standard is a day-one aim of 3–4 personal touches
(minimum 2 calls and 1 text), and a first-week target of 10 total including day one.
The quota ceases to apply once the buyer responds. Do not invent an exact late-day
schedule, voicemail counting rule, or post-break disposition workflow.

Do not repeat the interviews' unverified numerical outcome claims. Examples must
not imply confirmed access, a signed agreement, completed research, buyer consent,
or an agent affiliation without those facts. The touring-form mention is limited
to closing expectations; actual form terms and leadership judgment govern.
