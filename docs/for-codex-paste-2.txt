Supersedes the earlier version of this file from today. Eric reviewed the
first schema 1.2 batch in TRU HQ: pipeline, evidence fields, and validation
all worked. What follows came from a direct interview with him about the
REASONING, and it changes how conversations get tagged.

1. THE REASONING WALK-THROUGH — RUN PER AGENT, BEFORE TAGGING ANYTHING

The batch tagged "ended without a specific time" (next_steps) on 58 of its 92
opportunities, and "should have called" (call_first) twice across four teams.
Eric: "That's like the most generic of all of them... it is one of a host of
things." The analyst must reason through these, in order, for every agent:

  a. CHANNEL HABITS. Did they call or text first, per new lead, and at what
     rate? A Zillow message counts as a text.
  b. SENSITIVE SUBJECTS ON THE WRONG CHANNEL -> call_first. The must-call
     list, all four confirmed by Eric in interview:
       - a property no longer available (under contract, sold, off market,
         seller rejected). "An agent should never be texting them the
         properties under contract."
       - any disappointing update (price problems, inspection issues,
         financing trouble, appraisal gaps)
       - hyper-specific property discussion that has outgrown texting
       - money and finances, ALWAYS
  c. TONALITY — exactly two flags, no others. New pattern keys:
       - tone_rushed: calls where the agent hurries to end it, flat
         checked-out delivery. Quote the transcript; include durationSeconds.
       - tone_curt: curt, cold texting — one-word replies, abrupt messages.
         Quote the text. A short text that answers warmly is NOT curt.
     Eric was offered scripted-sounding delivery and talking-over-the-buyer
     and did NOT select them. Do not flag those.
  d. DOWN-FUNNEL LEADS WEIGH HEAVIER. A lead at the met-with stage or
     further down the funnel: a miss with them outranks the same miss on a
     cold lead. To support this, collection now also captures leadStage per
     conversation — the FUB stage as shown, verbatim; omit if not visible.
  e. Only after all of that: technique — the time ask, this-or-that, and the
     rest of the taxonomy.

2. PICK THE DOMINANT FAILURE — ONE OPPORTUNITY PER CONVERSATION

The channel outranks the close. If the subject was on the must-call list, or
the agent has NEVER called this lead at any point -> call_first (bad news
left sitting with no pivot -> negative_property_pivot). Stop there; do not
also mention the missing time offer. next_steps ONLY when the conversation
happened on the right channel and the sole miss was ending without a
specific time attempt.

Add to the final whole-payload consistency pass:
  - every next_steps sits on a right-channel conversation, else retag
  - no single patternKey is the majority of the batch; if one is, the
    walk-through was skipped — redo it on every opportunity before sending
  - every tone_rushed / tone_curt quotes verbatim words that show it, else drop

3. STRIP FUB CHROME FROM QUOTES

9 of 92 sourceQuotes carried Follow Up Boss interface text glued to the front
of the message, e.g. "Fernanda SilvaRicardo Gonzalez Aug 21 • Delivered Reply
<actual message>". A sourceQuote is the words the person said or typed,
nothing else. Check: no quote contains name+date headers, "Delivered",
"Reply", "Summary Transcript", or "Suggested Tasks" residue.

path. TRU HQ has hidden today's 1.1 reports and now displays the 1.2 batch; a
1.1 report arriving tomorrow would reappear as "latest" and put the old logic
back on screen.
