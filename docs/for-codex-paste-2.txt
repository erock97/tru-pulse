CODEX — THREE FIXES BEFORE THE NEXT DAILY RUN (contract addendum, 2026-08-26)

Eric reviewed the first schema 1.2 batch in TRU HQ. The pipeline, evidence
fields, and validation all worked. Three corrections for the analysis rules,
in priority order.

1. PICK THE DOMINANT FAILURE — THE CHANNEL OUTRANKS THE CLOSE

The batch tagged next_steps ("ended without a specific time") on 58 of its 92
opportunities, and call_first only twice across all four teams — on rosters
whose own metrics show texting dominating. "Ended without a time" fires on
every conversation that ends, so the analyst is tagging the easiest observable
miss on everything and burying the one that matters. Eric, on a John Blanquicet
point where a home going under contract was relayed by TEXT to a lead John had
never called: "Why is our feedback 'they should have offered specific times to
connect'? That is the smallest concern here."

New ranking rule, applied BEFORE choosing any patternKey. One conversation
gets ONE opportunity, tagged with the biggest miss:

  a. Channel first. Significant news delivered by text (a home going under
     contract, bad news of any kind, the buyer asking to talk), or a lead the
     agent has NEVER called at any point -> call_first. Bad news left sitting
     with no pivot to what's possible -> negative_property_pivot. Stop there —
     do not also mention the missing time offer.
  b. next_steps ONLY when the conversation happened on the right channel and
     the sole miss was ending without a specific time attempt.

Add two checks to the final whole-payload consistency pass:
  - every next_steps sits on a right-channel conversation, else retag
  - if next_steps is the majority of the whole batch, that is the signal the
    ranking was skipped — re-apply rule (a)/(b) to every opportunity before
    sending, never send as-is

2. STRIP FUB CHROME FROM QUOTES

9 of the batch's 92 sourceQuotes carry Follow Up Boss interface text glued to
the front of the real message, e.g. "Fernanda SilvaRicardo Gonzalez Aug 21 •
Delivered Reply <actual message>". The contract already requires stripping
thread headers at collection (section 1) — it needs to actually happen. A
sourceQuote is the words the person said or typed, nothing else. Add a check:
no quote contains names+date headers, "Delivered", "Reply", "Summary
Transcript", or "Suggested Tasks" residue.

3. CONFIRM THE 5AM SCHEDULE SWITCH

The daily 5:00 a.m. command must run the schema 1.2 workflow, not the old 1.1
path. Confirm the switch is live before tomorrow's run. TRU HQ has hidden
today's 1.1 reports and displays the 1.2 batch; a 1.1 report arriving tomorrow
would reappear as "latest" and put the old logic back on screen.

Everything else from the v2 contract stands unchanged. Mark runs schemaVersion
"1.2" as before — no version bump for this addendum.
