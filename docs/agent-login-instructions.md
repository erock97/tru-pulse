# Agent invitation and login instructions

Include this guidance in agent onboarding and training-access emails. In individual instructions, name the verified invitation recipient address. In group emails, tell each agent to use their own invitation address.

> Use the same email address that received your TRU HQ invitation whenever you sign in. That's the address connected to your Follow Up Boss profile, so your activity and reporting stay linked to you. If you sign in with Google, choose that same email address.

Reusable follow-up:

> Hey team,
>
> Check your inbox and spam folder for your TRU HQ invitation, then open the link to set your password. Use the same email address that received your invitation whenever you sign in. That's the address connected to your Follow Up Boss profile, so your activity and reporting stay linked to you. If you sign in with Google, choose that same email address.
>
> If you have trouble getting in, reply here and let me know. You don't need a FUB ID or API key to sign in.

Verify invite status before stating an invitation was sent. If an address is wrong or an account remains unlinked, investigate the invitation and roster rather than directing the agent to create a new team or provide FUB credentials.
