## Teaching and participation

Introduction and agenda precede instruction. Instruction and demonstrations precede related practice. Three multiple-choice discussions require explanations and consider what new information would change the recommendation. An open discussion requires two possible buyer answers and different recommendations. The 16-minute practice rotates agent/buyer/observer, includes feedback and retry, and records what was actually said. The final written activity checks whether the agreed plan supports a useful next contact.
