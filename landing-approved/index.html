    What we take on

    Work alongside leadership.
    Stay close to execution.

    Strategy matters when it changes what happens in the business. We help connect the plan to the agent, the conversation, and the follow-through.

    Put opportunities with the right people.

    Manage lead platforms, routing, agent capacity, and expansion plans. We help teams work the opportunities they receive, including realtor.com and Zillow Preferred buyer and seller leads.

    Work on the conversations that convert.

    Call review, objection handling, role-play, and buyer and seller processes. Give coaching a specific behavior to improve.

    Make follow-through visible.

    CRM adoption, pipeline management, follow-up standards, and performance reviews. Establish clear expectations and return to the work.

    Build leadership that can scale.

    Strengthen onboarding, coaching systems, and leadership meetings. Our experience spans teams of 5–8 agents through onboarding redesign for a team of 80+.
