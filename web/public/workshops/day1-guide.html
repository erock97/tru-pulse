Before you begin

Eric opens Rep, selects the training and existing agents across teams, confirms the timezone, and creates the session. The creator is the default presenter and follow-up coach. Keep the private presenter console on your screen and share the separate presentation window.

Ask each selected agent to open the session through their own existing login. No second invitation is needed. Confirm that everyone can join and see the current activity. Submit written work before showing the model. Short answers are ungraded: collect the learner’s reasoning without requiring corrections, keywords, or a retry to continue. Record exercises still check saved actions. Open activities under presenter control; advancing leaves unfinished work available for catch-up. Show help requests and connection problems separately.

In self-paced study, notes remain in the learner’s browser until they choose to share them. In a live session, the presenter sees submitted answers and progress; unsubmitted writing stays private. Preserve first attempts; a retry after an example is assisted.

For remote practice, use the meeting platform’s breakout rooms. Assign pairs or trios and rotate agent, buyer, and observer. In pairs, the buyer gives feedback. Every agent needs a speaking turn, one correction, and a retry. If a partner is absent, assign a coach or leave the observation outstanding.

Know the standards—and what they measure

Open a demonstration report only after removing private agent performance. Explain pCVR is not a promise that four of the next hundred close, and new agents should ask how their report is established. Example: 10 answered of 40 incoming attempts =25%; it is the minimum, not a target to miss the other calls. FUB use and current status reporting are required; team operational and seller-specific standards also apply to the enrolled team. Do not change stages to improve appearance.

4%+

Predicted conversion

Zillow’s estimate of future performance. It considers market results and your conversion and volume over 180 days, with recent 90-day activity weighted more.

25%+

Pickup rate

Incoming Zillow connection calls answered ÷ incoming connection call attempts over the last 90 days. This is not how often buyers answer your outbound calls.

Also understand your Zillow Home Loans pre-approval target: 100% of the assigned target over three months. Day 4 covers relevant introductions and buyer choice.

Define nurture: two different decisions

Give everyone quiet reading time and an independent response before discussion. For beginners, explain lead, stage, task, and automatic versus personal outreach using the facts on screen. Compare two different responses; ask “Which fact led you there?” rather than requiring a corrected answer. Explain the team’s actual nurture rule and why it applies. Do not invent a timeline or imply that a later move date or silence automatically means Nurture. Maya has an agreed call due; ask what the learner would clarify before changing the relationship. Jordan has two personal attempts in the first week, not three: the automatic message does not count. TRU’s first-week floor is at least five unique personal attempts from arrival; calls, including voicemail, and personal texts count. Do not turn that floor into a stop rule or impose a standard beyond week one. For experienced learners, use the changed-fact questions and ask what happens to an existing task if the stage changes. All written responses are ungraded; no rewrite is required to continue.

First, the terms: Nurture is a stage teams use for an ongoing relationship when their current active follow-up approach needs to change. Your coach will explain your team’s rule. Moving a name into a different stage does not, by itself, create a follow-up plan. A task is the dated reminder assigned to the person who will take the next action.

How it started: Maya requested a tour through Zillow three weeks ago. You spoke and toured one home together. She is renting until March and said, “I probably won’t move before my lease ends, but I want to understand the neighborhoods before I choose.”

What happened this week: On Tuesday she sent two listings and asked which area would make the commute easier. You agreed to call Friday at 4 p.m. She replied, “Yes, talk then.” She has not asked to stop hearing from you, and no second showing is booked.

What you inherit: The stage is Met with customer. A Friday 4 p.m. call task is assigned to you. A covering agent added, “Six months out — move to Nurture?” You have not discussed how often Maya wants contact or whether she would consider a suitable home sooner.

The decision: It is Friday at 3:45 p.m. Would you change the stage now, keep it while you clarify something, or take another step? What would you ask on the agreed call? Explain how your plan respects her timing and the promise already made.

Jordan Ellis · The record is quiet, but look at the attempts

How it started: Jordan requested information about 18 Cedar Street through Zillow on Monday morning. You called that morning, reached voicemail, and left a message offering to arrange a showing. You have never spoken with Jordan.

What happened this week: An automatic welcome text went out Monday. On Wednesday you personally texted, “I can help arrange a visit to Cedar Street — would Thursday afternoon or Saturday morning work?” There has been no reply. The record shows two property views on Thursday; it does not explain why Jordan viewed them.

What you inherit: It is Friday. The stage is Attempted contact. There is no next task. Another agent wrote, “No response — maybe not serious. Nurture?” The history contains one personal call and one personal text; the automatic message is separate.

The decision: What can you fairly conclude about Jordan’s interest? What would you do before treating this as a longer-term relationship? Choose a next attempt that offers a reason to respond, and explain when you would review the result. You do not know whether Jordan is busy, already helped, or no longer looking.

Ready for a harder question? How would your plan change if Maya asked for one call next month instead of Friday, or Jordan replied, “We bought yesterday”? Identify which new fact changes the action. Do not invent a universal number of days for moving someone to Nurture.
