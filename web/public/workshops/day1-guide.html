Before you begin

Keep this guide on a second screen or print it. Project the presentation window. Arrange pairs or trios and have paper available. Ask everyone to answer before revealing an example. After feedback, ask the agent to retry the specific part that needs work.

The timer supports short practice rounds. For longer rounds, use your room clock. Practice notes save on each device; they are not sent to the facilitator. Use hand votes or partner discussion for group responses.

Use the training records only. All five existing record exercises remain in the lesson. In learner mode, the record check must pass before continuing. Presenter mode allows free navigation. Exercise edits survive moving between slides during the same lesson; leaving or reloading resets the exercise. Saved written workshop notes are separate.

What to listen for

The agent can explain the event behind the stage, save and verify the change, write a factual note, and schedule the next action.

Facilitator notes

01 / 24 · Start · 2 min

Welcome to Zillow Preferred

Invite a recent example from one person, then begin. Keep the opening to two minutes.

Ask the room: Ask: What would you like to get better at today?

02 / 24 · Start · 3 min

What should the next agent know?

Ask everyone to choose before revealing. Hear two explanations. Keep this distinction for the later repair exercise.

Ask the room: What would you need before calling Avery?

03 / 24 · Start · 2 min

What we will practice over four days

Keep this overview brief. Ask which task an agent already does comfortably. Do not reteach later modules here.

Ask the room: Which part of your current process needs the most practice?

04 / 24 · Find the contact · 3 min

How a Zillow lead reaches you

Use your actual team routing process for the demonstration. Do not assume every notification means the same thing.

Ask the room: What would tell you that you have the correct record?

05 / 24 · Find the contact · 3 min

Start in People

Demonstrate People, smart lists, and filtering in your training account. Open the image at full size if labels are hard to see. Ask an agent to point to the source and most recent activity.

Ask the room: Where would you look for a new Zillow contact?

06 / 24 · Find the contact · 3 min

Find Avery in the training account

Have an agent narrate where they would click. Use the training account, never a live contact as a practice record.

Ask the room: Explain your first three clicks.

07 / 24 · Read the record · 3 min

Read before you change anything

Show a contact and allow 20 seconds of silent reading. Ask what is known and what still needs a question.

Ask the room: What would be risky to assume?

08 / 24 · Read the record · 3 min

Check the Details panel

Point out Stage, Assigned, Source, and Timeframe without narrating every control. Timing should come from the buyer, not a guess.

Ask the room: Which detail would you verify before saving?

09 / 24 · Read the record · 3 min

Separate facts from assumptions

Ask for two examples. Do not infer finances, family circumstances, or housing preferences from a profile. Keep the record factual.

Ask the room: Which words in a note tell you someone is guessing?

10 / 24 · Update the stage · 4 min

Choose the stage that matches the event

Show the full stage menu in the exercise. Explain this is the team’s stage mapping. A request for Thursday is not an appointment until a time is agreed. Demonstrate the green save check and reopening.

Ask the room: Which stage would you use after a voicemail?

11 / 24 · Update the stage · 3 min

Choose Avery’s stage

Ask agents to explain the evidence for their answer. Do not accept a label without the event behind it.

Ask the room: What new fact would justify Appointment set?

12 / 24 · Update the stage · 2 min

Check the other examples

Hide the answers first by asking verbally. Call on different agents. Next, each learner will make the appointment update in the existing record exercise.

Ask the room: Close your notes: what separates the last two?

13 / 24 · Record exercise · 5 min

Practice: save the appointment stage

Let the agent find the control before offering a hint. Ask them to explain any correction.

Ask the room: Make the update, save it, then check your work.

14 / 24 · Write the note · 3 min

Write what the next agent needs

Ask what the example tells the next caller. Then complete the note exercise. Coach one missing fact rather than rewriting the entire note for the agent.

Ask the room: Could someone follow up using only this note?

15 / 24 · Record exercise · 5 min

Practice: write the conversation note

Let the agent find the control before offering a hint. Ask them to explain any correction.

Ask the room: Make the update, save it, then check your work.

16 / 24 · Set the task · 3 min

Give the follow-up a date and time

Explain the difference between a note and a task. The note records history; the task schedules the next action. Complete the missed-call exercise next.

Ask the room: What makes a task usable tomorrow?

17 / 24 · Record exercise · 5 min

Practice: set the missed-call task

Let the agent find the control before offering a hint. Ask them to explain any correction.

Ask the room: Make the update, save it, then check your work.

18 / 24 · Practice · 3 min

Repair Avery’s record

Have agents diagnose first, then complete the repair exercise. Ask for evidence behind each change. The existing exercise checks the diagnosis before allowing edits.

Ask the room: What would you fix first, and why?

19 / 24 · Record exercise · 5 min

Practice: repair Avery’s record

Let the agent find the control before offering a hint. Ask them to explain any correction.

Ask the room: Make the update, save it, then check your work.

20 / 24 · Practice · 2 min

Check the record before moving on

Ask agents to check their own work. Introduce the deal exercise: once an offer is accepted, update the stage and record the deal details.

Ask the room: Which check would catch an unsaved stage?

21 / 24 · Record exercise · 3 min

Practice adding a deal

Demonstrate the deal form, then let the learner enter it. Use fictional details.

Ask the room: What would you check before creating this deal?

22 / 24 · Record exercise · 5 min

Practice: record the accepted offer

Check the accepted-offer exercise. A passed check should explain the evidence, not just display a result.

Ask the room: What changed after the offer was accepted?

23 / 24 · Retain · 3 min

Where will you start tomorrow?

Give 30 seconds without notes. Then ask partners to compare. Revisit only the missing item, not the entire lesson.

Ask the room: Explain your first five minutes in Follow Up Boss.

24 / 24 · Retain · 3 min

Before your next client conversation

Ask each agent to name one behavior they will practice. Within 24 hours, ask them to explain it without notes. In three days, use a new scenario. In seven days, review an actual example. These are suggested coaching intervals; no reminders are sent.

Ask the room: Tell your partner what you will practice and when.

Practice after the workshop

Within 24 hours, explain the lesson without notes and practice with a partner. In three days, try another buyer case. In seven days, review an actual example with your coach. These are suggested intervals, not scheduled reminders.
