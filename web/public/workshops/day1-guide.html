Before you begin

Eric opens Rep, selects the training and existing agents across teams, confirms the timezone, and creates the session. The creator is the default presenter and follow-up coach. Keep the presenter console on your screen and share the separate presentation window from the same browser. Click an answer on your presenter slide to highlight it in both windows. Ask for the reasoning, then click Submit answer to share the explanation. Open Presenter notes in the toolbar for the current slide; those notes stay private. People without HQ can answer aloud and explain their choice; this discussion selection does not create an individual submission.

Ask each selected agent to open the session through their own existing login. No second invitation is needed. Confirm that everyone can join and see the current activity. Submit written work before showing the model. Short answers are ungraded: collect the learner’s reasoning without requiring corrections, keywords, or a retry to continue. Record exercises still check saved actions. Advance to the question. Agents answer and submit on that slide, and their answers appear automatically beside your slide. Review actual arrivals before revealing the example. If someone needs more time, pause or return to the slide.

In self-paced study, notes remain in the learner’s browser until they choose to share them. In a live session, the presenter sees submitted answers and progress; unsubmitted writing stays private. Preserve first attempts; a retry after an example is assisted.

For remote practice, use the meeting platform’s breakout rooms. Assign pairs or trios and rotate agent, buyer, and observer. In pairs, the buyer gives feedback. Every agent needs a speaking turn, one correction, and a retry. If a partner is absent, assign a coach or leave the observation outstanding.

The program

Zillow Preferred is an invitation-only program, previously called Flex. Zillow connects you with potential buyers. A success fee applies when a transaction covered by your agreement closes. Review that agreement with your team.

Your role

Be available, understand the customer’s request, agree on a useful next step, and keep the record current. The customer may be new to buying and may not know who is calling.

Growth

Your performance and the areas you serve help determine how many leads you receive. Tell the team how many new leads you can handle each month. The limit is 15 per month, or 10 in your first month. You can ask for fewer. The maximum is not a guaranteed number of leads.

Define nurture: two different decisions

Give everyone quiet reading time and an independent response before discussion. For beginners, explain lead, stage, task, and automatic versus personal outreach using the facts on screen. Compare two different responses; ask “Which fact led you there?” rather than requiring a corrected answer. Explain the team’s actual nurture rule and why it applies. Do not invent a timeline or imply that a later move date or silence automatically means Nurture. Maya has an agreed call due; ask what the learner would clarify before changing the relationship. Jordan has two personal attempts in the first week, not three: the automatic message does not count. TRU’s first-week floor is at least five unique personal attempts from arrival; calls, including voicemail, and personal texts count.
