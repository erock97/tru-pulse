Before you begin

Keep the private presenter console on your screen and share the separate presentation window. Confirm the roster, team, signed-in account, invitation, training availability, and named coach. A join link identifies the session; each agent still needs authorization.

Ask each person to join through their own login. Submit written work before showing the model. Give one correction, then ask for a targeted retry. Open activities under presenter control; advancing leaves unfinished work available for catch-up. Show help requests and connection problems separately.

In self-paced study, notes remain in the learner’s browser until they choose to share them. In a live session, the presenter sees submitted answers and progress; unsubmitted writing stays private. Preserve first attempts; a retry after an example is assisted.

For remote practice, use the meeting platform’s breakout rooms. Assign pairs or trios and rotate agent, buyer, and observer. In pairs, the buyer gives feedback. Every agent needs a speaking turn, one correction, and a retry. If a partner is absent, assign a coach or leave the observation outstanding.

Follow Up Boss practice

The original five record exercises and deal form remain intact. Use fictional training records and preserve the repair diagnosis step. The simulator begins inside a contact; observe People/search navigation separately in the actual training account. A learner’s report does not automatically verify navigation.

Coach where to click, save, and verify. The server check validates saved actions; it does not judge note usefulness. After a passed record changes, check it again. Each exercise is a separate scenario. The 57-minute allocation is a pilot; extend practice rather than skipping attempts.

What each signal means

Connection status is separate from participation. A submitted answer is separate from a completed action. A record check is separate from writing quality. Record who observed a speaking turn and who reviewed it; a peer observation or self-check is not coach sign-off. Practice readiness, quiz certification, and activation remain separate.

10 / 17 · 25–33 min · Start with practice

Nurture needs a conversation

Give everyone two minutes to write before discussing. Compare two contrasting responses, then explain the team’s actual nurture rule and why it applies. Do not invent a timeline, assume silence means nurture, or impose a follow-up standard beyond the doctrine’s first-week rule. There is no automatic grade. Review the evidence and preserve an owned next action.

Ask the room: What evidence changes your decision, and what still needs to happen?

Participant task: Compare the engaged, later-timeline buyer with the record that went quiet.

  • For each case, what evidence would you need before moving this lead to nurture?
  • What next action would remain, and who owns it?
  • When would you review the record again?

Coach-led discussion. No automatic grade. Explain the team rule and ask for evidence; do not invent a nurture timeline.

Before your next client conversation

In live sessions, closing the session creates 24-hour recall, three-day fresh-case, and seven-day coach-review assignments. Confirm each agent’s named coach and session timezone. Agents see due work in Rep; the daily digest links to due or reviewable work. In self-paced study, arrange these checks with a coach. Without a relevant client opportunity, use a fresh fictional case and label real-work application not yet observed. Practice readiness is separate from quiz certification and activation.

Ask the room: Tell your partner what you will practice and when.

Participant task: Make the record update part of finishing the conversation.

  • After my next call, I will complete ___ before moving on.
  • My next follow-up block starts in ___.
  • My named coach and the example we will review.

After the workshop

Live session: closing the session creates assignments for recall within 24 hours, a fresh case in three days, and coach review in seven days. Confirm the named coach and displayed session timezone. Due and overdue work appears in Rep; the daily digest links to work due or awaiting review.

Self-paced study: arrange those same checks with a coach. If there has been no relevant client opportunity, use a new fictional case and mark real-work application as not yet observed.

Keep the first attempt and correction. A useful follow-up asks whether the agent can apply the skill in another case.
