Before you begin

Keep this guide on a second screen or print it. Project the presentation window. Arrange pairs or trios and have paper available. Ask everyone to answer before revealing an example. After feedback, ask the agent to retry the specific part that needs work.

The timer supports short practice rounds. For longer rounds, use your room clock. Practice notes save on each device; they are not sent to the facilitator. Use hand votes or partner discussion for group responses.

What to listen for

The agent connects the introduction to the buyer’s concern, explains a relevant benefit without promising an outcome, asks permission, and confirms the next action and time.

Facilitator notes

01 / 18 · Start · 2 min

Introducing buyers to Zillow Home Loans

Before arrival: open the Facilitator guide in a separate window or print it. Project only this training window. Have paper available. Explain what agents will practice; do not read a product agenda. Ask for a show of hands: who has lost momentum between a showing and a lender conversation? Invite one sentence, not a long story.

Ask the room: Ask the room: Where does financing usually stall?

02 / 18 · Start · 3 min

What would you ask Jordan?

Give everyone 20 seconds to choose silently. Ask for a hand vote before selecting an answer. Invite someone from each choice to explain. Listen for assumptions about affordability and approval. Revisit Jordan in the final check; this is the thread through the workshop.

Ask the room: Commit to an answer before the reveal.

03 / 18 · Understand · 3 min

Pre-qualification, pre-approval, and final approval

Ask agents to explain the stages without reading them. Correct the idea that a branded letter is a guarantee. Do not claim that all other lenders use unverified information. Fully underwritten pre-approval is another level of review to discuss with the loan officer. Ask: what would you want to know before relying on any letter?

Ask the room: Partner prompt: Explain the difference in one sentence.

04 / 18 · Understand · 3 min

What Zillow Home Loans offers your buyer

Keep the ecosystem explanation to one minute. Ask: which of these benefits matters to Jordan right now? Accept a buyer-specific answer. Remove the implication that software removes all friction or that a particular letter automatically wins an offer. A named loan officer and a clear next action are the practical value.

Ask the room: Ask: Which benefit would Jordan actually care about?

05 / 18 · Understand · 3 min

Questions for the loan officer

The source deck’s 585 floor conflicts with the current FAQ, which states 620 for most loan types. Do not replace one blanket cutoff with another in agent scripts. Route eligibility to the lender. The FAQ contains inconsistent wording about hard checks, but explicitly allows circumstances requiring a hard inquiry. Do not use “soft pull always.” Keep product exclusions, exact terms, fee claims and special pricing in the reference review, not memory drills.

Ask the room: Quick check: Who confirms eligibility and the credit inquiry?

06 / 18 · Understand · 2 min

How would you explain the credit check?

Pause before clicking. Ask agents to rewrite one of the wrong answers in their own voice. Take two answers. Move on once they can state a benefit without a promise. If most miss it, return to the preceding slide for a 30-second clarification.

Ask the room: No notes: rewrite one of the other sentences.

07 / 18 · Connect · 3 min

How to introduce your loan officer

Model the four steps with your own words before advancing. Have the room say the four verbs once. Explain that buyer choice is part of a good conversation, not an objection-handling trick. Avoid hiding the lender’s identity behind a first name.

Ask the room: Listen for all four steps in the example that follows.

08 / 18 · Connect · 3 min

Example: introducing Jordan to a loan officer

Read once at a conversational pace, with pauses. Then ask: what made that relevant to Jordan? Where was the choice? The last question is conditional on consent; if they decline, do not jump to scheduling. Ask one volunteer to paraphrase the entire conversation without looking.

Ask the room: Ask: Where does Jordan get to choose?

09 / 18 · Connect · 5 min

Practice your introduction

Give 30 seconds to draft. Each partner gets 60 seconds to speak, 30 seconds of feedback, and a 30-second targeted retry. Swap speakers and repeat. Use the final 30 seconds to share one improvement. Avoid polishing every word as a group; practice the behavior.

Ask the room: Practice out loud. Reading silently does not count.

10 / 18 · Practice · 4 min

Responding to common concerns

Have an agent answer before revealing each coaching example. Ask the buyer role: did that address your actual concern? If not, ask a question instead of pushing harder. Cover all three briefly. On comparison shopping, do not promise to beat a rate or say a comparison is automatically free of credit impact.

Ask the room: Choose an objection. Let the room answer before revealing.

11 / 18 · Practice · 3 min

Make the introduction and confirm the call

Ask: if we send this and hear nothing, who owns the next move? The agent owns checking that the introduction landed, not promising a loan outcome. Do not put financial documents or sensitive account information into a group message. Use the approved lender channel for the application.

Ask the room: Ask: Where do handoffs disappear on our team?

12 / 18 · Practice · 8 min

Practice with a buyer and an observer

Allow 1 minute to assign roles. Run three 2-minute rounds: 60 seconds conversation, 30 seconds specific feedback, 30 seconds retry. Rotate roles and practice case. Give 1 minute to debrief. The observer scores observed behavior, not confidence or personality. Success is all four behaviors, without unsupported claims. If someone misses one, have them repeat that exact portion. Pairs alternate and self-score if needed.

Ask the room: Every agent speaks. Every agent gets a second attempt.

13 / 18 · Apply · 3 min

Follow up on incomplete introductions

The original deck specifies 4% conversion, 25% pickup, 10% pre-approval, and a rolling 90-day window. Those exact thresholds, attribution rules, and lead consequences were not established by current public sources. Confirm them in the team’s live dashboard with the program owner. Explain numerator and denominator using the example, without presenting it as a policy. Do not imply agents control approval decisions or should change statuses to improve a metric.

Ask the room: Ask: Which stalled introduction can you move forward?

14 / 18 · Apply · 3 min

Check financing before writing an offer

Give 30 seconds to answer before revealing. Invite one person to act as Jordan and another as the agent. Ask Jordan to repeat what happens next; if they cannot, the agent should clarify the handoff. Reinforce that a lender’s average speed is not a promise for this transaction.

Ask the room: Ask the buyer role to repeat the next step back.

15 / 18 · Retain · 3 min

What do you remember?

Give 60 seconds with no help. Have partners compare from memory for 30 seconds. Reveal and ask them to fill a gap in a different color on paper, or revise their text. This revisits key ideas after a delay for retrieval, not another lecture. If a gap is common, ask for a fresh example instead of rereading.

Ask the room: Recall first. Reveal after everyone has tried.

16 / 18 · Retain · 3 min

How would you answer Jordan?

Vote before selecting. Ask someone who chose correctly to explain why the other answers fall short. Finish with a 30-second individual spoken response. The multiple-choice answer is only a check; the spoken response demonstrates the skill. Use the four-behavior rubric if an agent needs another attempt.

Ask the room: Ask: Why would Jordan be concerned about this?

17 / 18 · Retain · 3 min

Plan your next practice

Give 30 seconds to write a commitment with a named practice partner and time. Have agents share with their partner. The follow-up schedule is a suggested coaching routine, not an automated reminder. A coach should ask for a real example of changed behavior next week. Invite remaining questions; move product-specific questions to the loan officer.

Ask the room: Say your commitment to your practice partner.

18 / 18 · Finish · 1 min

What to do after this training

Close on the behavior, not the team metric. Ask for one final takeaway in the buyer’s language. Keep the resource sheet available for questions. If an agent did not demonstrate all four behaviors, schedule another brief practice before marking the skill ready in your own coaching process. This workshop does not issue a certification.

Ask the room: Final prompt: What will sound different on your next call?

Buyer practice cards

Clarify readiness and ask permission for a useful next step.

Morgan · already has a lender

My bank has handled everything for years. Why would I need another conversation?

Acknowledge the relationship. Explore whether a comparison would help.

Alex · credit concern

I want to understand the credit check before deciding.

Respect the concern. Let the loan officer explain the actual process.

Practice after the workshop

Within 24 hours, explain the lesson without notes and practice with a partner. In three days, try another buyer case. In seven days, review an actual example with your coach. These are suggested intervals, not scheduled reminders.
