Pre-qualification, pre-approval, and final approval

These are different stages. Ask what has actually been checked.

Pre-qualification

Zillow describes this as an estimate using self-reported information and a soft credit check. Ask what the buyer has actually completed.

Pre-approval

The lender reviews supporting information and identifies conditions. Ask the loan officer what has been verified and what remains before relying on the letter.

Final approval

The lender must resolve the remaining borrower and property requirements. A pre-approval letter does not guarantee funding. Confirm readiness and timing with the loan officer.

Zillow Home Loans FAQ, reviewed September 16, 2026. Ask the lender to explain credit inquiries for this buyer before they proceed.

Questions for the loan officer

Know which questions need a loan officer’s answer.

Before the buyer proceeds

“What will you review at this step? Will it involve a soft or hard inquiry, and what should the buyer expect afterward?”

Before relying on a letter

“What has been verified? What documents or conditions remain? Does this property change the review or payment?”

Before committing to timing

“What can you support on this file, and what do you need first?” Give the buyer the confirmed answer rather than a general estimate.

Jordan wants to make an offer

Suggested choice: Clarify what the bank confirmed and offer help.

Clarify the buyer’s actual need before offering a relevant option. Explain one supported benefit, preserve lender choice, and confirm the next action only after consent. An introduction is optional; approval and savings cannot be promised.

Jordan, you want to be ready to offer, and we still need to confirm the financing. I work with a loan officer at Zillow Home Loans who can help you understand your options and next steps. Would an introduction be useful? You’re free to choose the lender that works best for you. [If accepted:] Is a call this afternoon a useful next step?

What would you clarify about the buyer’s financing?

Ask what the lender has reviewed, what is still conditional, whether the property affects the review, and what timing the file can support. Let the loan officer confirm the process for this buyer. Avoid promising approval, credit impact or a closing date.

How would you explain the credit check?

Suggested choice: “Pre-qualification uses a soft check. Let’s ask the loan officer what happens after that.”

Correct. Explain the initial step, then let the loan officer confirm what this buyer’s process requires.

What would you ask when the buyer already has a lender?

Acknowledge the existing relationship. Ask whether anything remains unclear. Offer a second conversation only if it would help. Identify Zillow Home Loans clearly, ask permission and respect a decline.

Responding to common concerns

Already has a lender: “What do you like about working with them, and is there anything you still want clarity on?” If useful, offer an optional second perspective without promising savings. Credit concern: “Is your concern about the first step, or about what happens later in the application?” Let the loan officer explain the process; do not promise every step is free of credit impact. Required lender: “You choose your lender. My role is to help you understand your options. An introduction is available if you want it.” If they decline, respect it and discuss the next step with their chosen lender.

What would you do after an unanswered introduction?

Check that the loan officer received the introduction and confirm a workable contact time with both parties. Tell the buyer what to expect. Record permission, the next owner and the follow-up time. Sending a message alone does not confirm the handoff.

Check financing before writing an offer

“The loan officer is checking the details for this property and what is still needed. Let’s confirm those items and a workable timeline before we rely on financing terms in the offer.”

What do you remember?

Reflect → Explain → Ask → Confirm. A pre-approval is conditional; it does not guarantee funding. A complete handoff includes permission, an identified loan officer, a confirmed next step and owner, and a follow-up time.
