Buyer practice cards

Jordan · weekend showing

“I asked about Lane because I need a quieter space to work. Saturday morning might work.”

Buyer: ask whether the agent is the listing agent. Agent: use LEAD and answer honestly.

Morgan · property question

“Before I book anything, do you know how old the roof is?”

Buyer: accept another time if the first two do not work. Agent: say what you know, capture what needs checking, and offer a visit.

Alex · home unavailable

“I only called about that one home. Is it still available?”

Practice fact: the home is under contract. Tell the buyer promptly and ask whether similar homes would help.
