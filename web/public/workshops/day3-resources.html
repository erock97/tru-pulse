Buyer practice cards

Jordan · ready to offer

“The second house is a nine. I would like to offer, but I have not sent my lender the documents.”

Learn why it is a nine. Clarify preparation and agree on a useful next step.

Morgan · fixed deadline

“The layout was good, but neither had the outdoor space I need. My lease ends November 30.”

Learn the missing criteria and whether the date has flexibility. Agree on the next search and appointment.

Alex · flexible timing

“Nothing felt quite right. I said six months, but I am not working toward a deadline.”

Accept the answer. Learn what was missing and agree on a follow-up the buyer finds useful.
