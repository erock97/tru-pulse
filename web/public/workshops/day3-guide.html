Before you begin

Eric opens Rep, selects the training and existing agents across teams, confirms the timezone, and creates the session. The creator is the default presenter and follow-up coach. Keep the presenter console on your screen and share the separate presentation window from the same browser. Click an answer on your presenter slide to highlight it in both windows. Ask for the reasoning, then click Submit answer to share the explanation. Open Presenter notes in the toolbar for the current slide; those notes stay private. People without HQ can answer aloud and explain their choice; this discussion selection does not create an individual submission.

Ask each selected agent to open the session through their own existing login. No second invitation is needed. Confirm that everyone can join and see the current activity. Submit written work before showing the model. Give one correction, then ask for a targeted retry. Advance to the question. Agents answer and submit on that slide, and their answers appear automatically beside your slide. Review actual arrivals before revealing the example. If someone needs more time, pause or return to the slide.

In self-paced study, notes remain in the learner’s browser until they choose to share them. In a live session, the presenter sees submitted answers and progress; unsubmitted writing stays private. Preserve first attempts; a retry after an example is assisted.

For remote practice, use the meeting platform’s breakout rooms. Assign pairs or trios and rotate agent, buyer, and observer. In pairs, the buyer gives feedback. Every agent needs a speaking turn, one correction, and a retry. If a partner is absent, assign a coach or leave the observation outstanding.

Rehearse without agents

Create a new test session for this day without selecting agents. Open the test learner in a second tab. Advance to a vote, submit a choice, and review it in the presenter console. Reveal the example and check that it reaches the learner. Repeat with a short answer. At a breakout, choose Assign speaking turns, speak a turn, save a presenter observation, and check the feedback in the learner tab. These responses are saved only as test evidence; ending the test creates no agent assignments. Existing sessions keep their original curriculum; create a new session for this edition.

What each signal means

Connection status is separate from participation. A submitted answer is separate from a completed action. A record check is separate from writing quality. Record who observed a speaking turn and who reviewed it; a peer observation or self-check is not coach sign-off. Practice readiness, quiz certification, and activation remain separate.

12 / 20 · 32–36 min · Help the buyer solve the problem

Buyer practice cards

Case 1 · The home does not fit

“We liked the living area, but I could hear everything from the office. We are still figuring out what matters.”

Buyer facts: one buyer takes work calls at home; a quiet enclosed office matters more than an extra bedroom. They want to compare two quieter layouts next Saturday if access works. Reveal the reason when asked. Agent: learn what “quiet” requires and agree on a search and confirmation plan; do not promise access.

Case 2 · The timing has a reason

“We like the home, but we keep saying a year. I do not know what payment we would be comfortable with.”

Buyer facts: a year is an estimate, not a fixed deadline. They have not spoken to a lender and want information before deciding whether buying fits. They are open to an introduction, not committed to buying. Agent: clarify the concern, offer relevant preparation, and agree how to reconnect without accelerating their decision.

Case 3 · An answer is holding up the decision

“This one could work, but the roof worries me. I need to understand that before deciding whether to take the next step.”

Buyer facts: the roof age is unknown. They would like to consider an offer soon if they can understand the issue, but have not decided to offer. They can talk again at six. Agent: establish what information matters and how urgent it is, seek appropriate help, and agree on an update without promising the outside answer.
